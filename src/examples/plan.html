Try the whole review loop.

  1. Connect your agent

    Expand the floating bar. Copy its connection command into your agent chat. Wait for Agent listening.

  2. Point and comment

    Enable commenting in the bar. Click an element to leave feedback. A numbered marker keeps it attached.

  3. Edit the wording

    Click plain text while commenting is on. Type your change, then press Enter to confirm its local preview.

  4. Send one review

    Add this plan and any comments to your feedback. Choose Send to deliver them together to your agent.

Text edits preview locally. Your agent applies source changes and acknowledges the review when finished.

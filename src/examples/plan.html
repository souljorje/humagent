Humagent Interactive demo

Show your agent what you mean.

Point at any part of a page, rewrite its text, and answer its questions. Humagent collects all of it into one review for your agent.

  1. Connect

    Expand the bar at the bottom. Paste its wait command into your agent chat.

  2. Review

    Turn on commenting in the bar. Click any element on this page to comment on it, or click its text to rewrite it.

  3. Send

    Add an overall instruction if you want. Choose Send to deliver everything as one review.

Plan your next change.

Agents can ask for structured choices. Your answers join the review as you change them.

Use it on your own pages.

Run humagent open ./page.html for an HTML file, or humagent open http://localhost:3000 for a running local app.

Enter confirms an edit. Shift and Enter add a new line. Escape cancels. Arrow keys move between elements while commenting.

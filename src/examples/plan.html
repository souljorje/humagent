Humagent Interactive demo

Show your agent what you mean.

Point at any part of a page, rewrite its text, and answer its questions. Humagent collects all of it into one review for your agent. Everything on this page is a live target. Try it now with the bar at the bottom.

  1. Connect

    Expand the bar at the bottom. Paste its wait command into your agent chat.

  2. Review

    Turn on commenting with the bar's comment button. Then try each section below.

  3. Send

    Add an overall instruction if you want. Choose Send to deliver everything as one review.

Plan your next change.

Agents can ask for structured choices. Your answers join the review as you change them, and the bar lists them with their labels.

What should the agent change first?

Choose Send in the floating bar when your review is ready.

Your review survives the refresh.

  • Reload this page. Unsent comments, edits, and answers return.
  • Open the session in another tab. Both tabs share one review.
  • If a send fails, your drafts stay saved. Send again when ready.

Your agent gets one structured review.

Each Send becomes one event. Your agent reads every comment, edit, and answer in order. It applies the changes to the source, then acknowledges the review. Text previews clear when it does.

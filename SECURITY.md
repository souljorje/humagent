# Security policy

Humagent serves trusted local pages and development apps on loopback only. It is not a host for multiple users and not a sandbox for malicious websites.

Report vulnerabilities through [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/souljorje/humagent/security/advisories/new). Do not open a public issue.

Include the affected version, a reproduction, and the possible impact. Do not include secrets or private files.

The maintainer reviews reports and coordinates a fix. See [Security and limits](docs/SECURITY.md) for the design and its limits.
